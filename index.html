What a full review is

A full review starts from £95, depending on the complexity of the issues found in the free overview.

It focuses on your homepage because many common website issues start there and are often repeated across the wider site.

The findings can then be applied across service pages, offer pages, and other key content.

This is not a redesign. It is a practical review that shows what is wrong, why it matters, and what to improve first.

Implementing the changes

A Site Clarity review focuses on pages, content and features that already exist.

In many cases, the recommendations are changes your team may already have the skills to make.

The report is written to help you understand the issue, why it matters, and what needs to change next.

You can use the report yourself, share it with your developer, or pass it to the person who manages your website content.

If you would like extra support, you can also work with one of the Site Clarity team to talk through the report and decide what to do first.

Issues we may find

Most website problems are small on their own, but together they can stop people using the page properly.

A review may identify issues such as:

  • Broken links or buttons
  • Navigation that makes key pages hard to find
  • Content that is unclear or hard to scan
  • Mobile layout problems
  • Poor colour contrast
  • Accessibility issues that may affect WCAG compliance
  • Page structure problems that affect users and search visibility
  • Key actions that are easy to miss

The review explains which issues matter most and what to improve first.

Why these issues matter

Website issues can make people leave, lose trust, or fail to take action.

For public sector organisations, accessibility issues can affect WCAG compliance and make services harder to use.

For small businesses, usability issues can affect enquiries, bookings, sales, and search visibility.

A review helps you understand what is getting in the way and what to improve first.
